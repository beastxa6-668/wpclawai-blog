## Your quiz answers stay in your browser

Find Your Toy runs on this page. It does not ask for a name, email address, account, or other identifying information. Answers are held in memory while the page is open, are not saved to browser storage, and are not sent to us. Reloading the page clears them.

## Links and hosting

Product links contain campaign labels that identify the quiz or article placement, not your answers. Following a product link opens SinDept, which has its own privacy policy. The website's hosting provider may process ordinary connection information when serving pages and images.
