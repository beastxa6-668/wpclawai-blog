**How to use a vibrator:** read its instructions, learn how to turn it off, and start with the lowest setting on an area the device is designed for. Keep the pressure light and change one setting at a time. Stop if anything hurts, feels numb or irritates your skin. You do not need to work through every mode—or achieve a particular result.

## What should I check before switching it on?

Charge a rechargeable device as directed, then disconnect it from the charger before use. Locate the power button and practise turning the device on and off before placing it against your body. Some controls cycle through patterns; others change speed separately. Knowing which is which makes the first session less fiddly.

Clean the contact surface according to the instructions. Check for cracks, exposed wiring or damaged surfaces, and set a damaged device aside. Put the instructions somewhere you can find them again; a waterproof rating and a charging symbol answer different questions.

If you are still choosing rather than using a device you already own, begin with our [first-vibrator buying guide](/guides/how-to-choose-your-first-vibrator/).

## How do I use a vibrator without starting too strongly?

Start with a low setting and light contact. Increasing pressure and speed together makes it hard to tell which change you like. Try adjusting one thing, pause, and decide whether you want more, less or a break.

## Do I need lubricant?

Use a water-based lubricant when the product instructions call for it or when it helps reduce friction. Check compatibility with the device and any barrier you use. More pressure is not the answer to uncomfortable friction.

Avoid using a toy on broken or irritated skin. Pain, bleeding or persistent numbness are reasons to stop and seek medical advice, rather than try a different setting. A device should not be used to diagnose or treat a health concern.

## Can I rinse it or use it in the bath?

Check the individual device's water-resistance instructions. The cat-shaped suction toy above lists IPX7; that rating does not transfer to the wand simply because both recharge. Keep charging connections dry and follow the manufacturer's cleaning directions. Never use these rechargeable models while charging.

## How should I clean and store it afterward?

Follow the product's care instructions before and after use. Let the device dry completely before putting it away separately in a clean, dry place. Our [cleaning and storage guide](/guides/how-to-clean-and-store-your-toys/) explains how material and electronics affect the routine.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I use every pattern on the first try?

No. One comfortable setting is enough. You can explore other controls another time.

### Is a wand the same thing as a suction toy?

No. A wand delivers vibration through its head; an air-pulse toy delivers focused pressure pulses. Compare their intended contact areas and controls before choosing.

### What if I do not like the first device I try?

That tells you something about your preferences, not your body. Consider whether the shape, contact area, controls or sensation was the mismatch before buying another device.
