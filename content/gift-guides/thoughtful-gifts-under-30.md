Nora had set a thirty-dollar gift budget before she remembered shipping. At the kitchen table, she moved a ribbon spool aside and opened the calculator on her phone.

“Thirty for the thing,” Alex said, “or thirty by the time it gets here?”

“That is suddenly a very good question.”

They had already agreed that an intimate gift could be welcome. They hadn't agreed on which kind. Alex wanted something straightforward; Nora had been comparing features neither of them had asked for.

She crossed out “most impressive” on her paper and wrote “would actually want.” Then she added a separate line for delivery. They left the ribbon where it was and made coffee. Choosing together still left room for a present. It just removed the part where one person had to pretend to like a surprise.

## Start with one question, not a shopping cart

Ask: “Would you enjoy an intimate gift, and would you rather choose it yourself?” A yes to the category isn't a yes to every design or an agreement to use it together.

If you'd prefer a surprise, agree on the type, spending limit and anything to avoid. A small handwritten note or choosing the wrapping can provide the surprise without making the product a guess.

For help finding the words, see [how to bring up trying something new](/relationships/how-to-bring-up-trying-something-new/).

## Make the handoff easy, too

Confirm where the recipient wants the parcel delivered. Plain outer packaging keeps product details off the outside; shared email, tracking alerts and the arrival of a parcel still deserve thought. ## FAQ

### Are all three gifts under $30 including shipping?

No. All three item prices are below $30. The table separates product prices from shipping, and applicable tax is added at checkout.

### Which is the simplest option if we don't want an app?

Compare the cordless wand for broad body massage with the single-button dual ring for someone who specifically wants that wearable shape. Start with the desired type of contact, not the number of settings.

### Can I buy an intimate gift without asking first?

Ask first unless you've already agreed that this kind of surprise is welcome. A low price doesn't remove the need to discuss preferences and boundaries.

### Should I buy something extra for free shipping?

Only if you independently want that item. Compare your actual basket total with the delivery charge; spending more to reach $99 can defeat a modest gift budget.
