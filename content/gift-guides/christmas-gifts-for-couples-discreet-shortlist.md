## Is an app-controlled ring a good choice for beginners?

It can fit a couple who already wants that format and is comfortable with the controls. Fit comes first: a flexible ring should still be comfortable to put on and remove. A general size label is not a substitute for checking the product's fit guidance.

Learn how to switch it off and remove it before using it together. Stop and remove a ring immediately if there is pain, numbness, coldness or a change in skin color; do not sleep in it. Seek urgent medical help if it becomes stuck. If you have a circulation condition or a relevant medical concern, ask a clinician before using a constricting product.

You can also choose a non-wearable option. Nobody needs to start with a ring just because the gift is for two people.

## What should I ask about app privacy?

Check whether the app requires an account, which permissions it requests and how partner access is granted and revoked. Agree who controls the device and how either person can end a session. For a recipient who prefers fewer connected features, compare a local remote or ordinary device controls instead.

The policy says plain outer packaging without product descriptions on the outside. That protects against casual recognition, not against every way a purchase can become visible. A shared email account, tracking alert or parcel collected by a housemate can still reveal that a delivery is arriving.
