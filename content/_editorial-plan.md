# From week 2 onward

- Monday: Toy Guides — comparisons, first-purchase checklists, materials, cleaning, sizing and care.
- Wednesday: Sex & Pleasure or Ask Velvet — one practical question or skill; a story opening may introduce the problem, but the article resolves it with useful guidance.
- Friday: Gift Ideas or Relationships — gifts by budget, occasion or recipient, or a communication/connection guide.
- Pure fiction: one story every two weeks, following the SinDept late-night story cadence. Stories stay under `/stories/` and are labeled FICTION.
- Realistic Dolls: never a blog category or quiz result.

The homepage gives practical guides the front-door position. Stories remain discoverable in Latest and relevant tags without being the primary navigation.
